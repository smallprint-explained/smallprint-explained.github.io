If you email us, we process your email address, name (if given) and the content of your message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). The mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail). We delete enquiries when they are settled unless we must keep them for legal reasons.

4. Our social media channels

We publish videos on YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. When you watch, like, comment on or follow our content there, the respective platform processes your data under its own privacy policy: Google/YouTube, TikTok, Instagram/Meta. We can see public comments and the aggregated, anonymous statistics the platforms provide to channel owners (for example views, watch time, and the age range or country of the audience as a whole). We use these statistics only to decide which topics and formats work better. We do not receive or store personal profiles of individual viewers.

5. SMALL PRINT publishing tool and API services

SMALL PRINT uses a small private software tool (“SMALL PRINT Publisher”) to upload its own videos, captions and descriptions to its own accounts and to read the statistics of its own channels. The tool is used only by the operator, for the operator’s own accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else’s account.

YouTube API Services

The tool uses the YouTube API Services. By using it, the account holder agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s processing of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. The tool accesses only the SMALL PRINT YouTube channel: it uploads videos and captions, updates their metadata and reads channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API). Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page.

TikTok and Instagram

Where enabled, the tool uses the TikTok Content Posting API and the Instagram Graph API (Meta) in the same way: only to publish SMALL PRINT’s own videos to SMALL PRINT’s own accounts and to read their aggregated statistics. Access can be revoked in the settings of the respective account.

What the tool stores

The tool stores the access tokens issued by these services and the statistics it reads locally on the operator’s own computer. It does not sell, share or transfer this data to anyone, does not use it for advertising and does not combine it with other data. Tokens are deleted when access is revoked or the tool is no longer used; statistics are kept as long as needed to plan the channel and reviewed at least yearly.
